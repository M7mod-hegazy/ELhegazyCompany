## Design Context

### Users
Egyptian shop owners, small-business teams, and growing local brands—often browsing on mobile and sometimes on slow or unstable connections. They need to understand quickly what ElHegazi offers, see credible proof from real Egyptian commerce, and start a conversation through WhatsApp without learning technical language.

### Brand Personality
Grounded, capable, and distinctly Egyptian. The voice is direct and practical, with the confidence of a business that uses its own POS, commerce, and marketing systems before selling them to others. The experience should feel premium without becoming distant or corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
“Engraved brass on ink”: museum-grade near-black surfaces, warm brass details, bone text, sharp ruled rectangles, and a restrained cinematic rhythm. The brass seal is the signature circular motif. Avoid generic SaaS cards, glassmorphism, neon gradients, decorative motion, and rounded containers.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the business outcome in familiar Egyptian language; explain technology second.
2. Prove every promise with real shops, screens, numbers, or client work.
3. Treat slow mobile connections as a primary context: posters first, adaptive video, resilient images, and interruptible navigation.
4. Keep Arabic-first RTL behavior complete while maintaining equally clear English.
5. Make WhatsApp the shortest path to action, with specific pre-filled messages tied to the visitor’s goal.
6. Use one purposeful cinematic gesture per moment, always respecting reduced motion and never blocking control.
