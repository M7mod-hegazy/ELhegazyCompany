📥 PROJECT INBOX

Drop photos for a new project here — any names, any order.

Then message your AI:
"Add a project: [name, client, year, type, what you did, the result, link]"

The AI moves the photos into the project folder, names them 01.jpg, 02.jpg, …
(the first one becomes the cover), and writes info.json for you.

Nothing in this folder ever appears on the site.
